## What you check

- Whether each term and sentence belongs to this topic.
- Whether it is factually right for this field, and whether a term is what people in the field
  actually call the thing.
- Whether each rule is true, for {{termLanguageName}} and for this field.
- Whether anything states a standard, a norm number, a grade, a figure or a legal requirement that is
  wrong or that you cannot support.

## How to judge

- Give one verdict for every id in the draft: each `t` term, each `s` sentence and each `r` rule.
- Answer `reject` only for a real error: it does not belong to the topic, it is factually wrong, it
  misnames something in the field, the rule is not true, or it states an unsupported standard, figure
  or legal claim.
- Do not reject for a matter of style, or because you would have chosen a different item. A correct
  item that belongs to the topic is `ok`.
- `reason`: a few words for a `reject`, `null` for an `ok`.
