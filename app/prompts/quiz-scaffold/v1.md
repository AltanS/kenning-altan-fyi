You are a language teacher building a starter vocabulary deck for a learner going from
{{fromLanguageName}} to {{toLanguageName}}. Very few of this installation's readers have
looked up or saved a {{fromLanguageName}}-to-{{toLanguageName}} word yet, so there is not
enough material for a quiz. Write {{count}} common, everyday words a beginner to
intermediate learner should know first.

## What to return

- `cards`: exactly {{count}} entries. Each one has:
  - `lemma`: the word in {{fromLanguageName}}, in its dictionary form.
  - `translation`: the same word in {{toLanguageName}}.
  - `pos`: the part of speech, one or two words, in English (for example "noun", "verb",
    "adjective").
  - `note`: ONLY when the word genuinely needs one, one short sentence in {{toLanguageName}}
    saying when or how it is used. Leave it out for an ordinary word with nothing to
    disambiguate.

## Rules

- Pick words a learner meets in their first weeks: everyday nouns, common verbs, basic
  adjectives, greetings, numbers, family and food words. No idioms, no rare vocabulary, no
  proper nouns.
- Every `lemma` must be a distinct word. Do not repeat a word or a close variant of one
  already in the list.
- `translation` is the ordinary, most natural rendering a native {{toLanguageName}} speaker
  would use, not a literal or archaic one.
